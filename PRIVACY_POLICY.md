# Privacy Policy for Hort Ballistic Calculator

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

Hort Ballistic Calculator ("we", "our", or "app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use our Garmin Connect IQ application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
* **Local Storage:** The app stores user-entered ballistic profiles (rifle parameters, ammunition data, zero offsets, and environmental settings) locally on your Garmin device using standard Connect IQ Storage API. This data is never transmitted to external servers.
* **Licensing & Payment Data:** To verify trial status and licensing, the app uses the KiezelPay service. During license checks, the app securely transmits a unique device identifier (`deviceId`) to KiezelPay servers (`api.kiezelpay.com`). No personal user identity, names, or location data are linked or sent during this process.

## 2. Third-Party Services
We use KiezelPay as a third-party payment and licensing processing system. You can view KiezelPay's privacy policy at: https://kiezelpay.com/privacy

## 3. Location Data & Sensors
The app may access your device's built-in sensors (barometer, temperature, elevation) solely for real-time ballistic calculations on the watch interface. This data is processed locally and is never stored permanently or shared.
